About the Iroko Framework

The Iroko Framework provides controlled vocabularies and access governance mechanisms for documenting sacred knowledge in Afro-Atlantic traditions. It supports postcustodial archival approaches where communities maintain authority over their knowledge while enabling semantic interoperability.

All modules serialize to RDF/Turtle and integrate with standard external vocabularies including Darwin Core, Dublin Core, PROV-O, FOAF, schema.org, and OntoLex-Lemon.

  • Field-Level Access Control — Granular permissions from public-unrestricted through initiated-elder, applied at the property level
  • Contested Knowledge Support — Multiple lineage perspectives coexist through RelationshipAssertion reification without forcing resolution
  • Provenance Tracking — Oral transmission chains, fieldwork context, epistemic transparency throughout
  • Semantic Interoperability — Integrates Darwin Core, SKOS, Dublin Core, PROV-O, FOAF, schema.org, OntoLex-Lemon
