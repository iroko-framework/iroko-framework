Contested Authority

https://iroko-framework.github.io/iroko-framework/vocab/iroko-core#ContestedAuthority
CLASS

An authority (religious title, position, or role) whose legitimacy or historical continuity is disputed across lineages. Example: Iyanifa (women Ifa priests) - some lineages affirm historical continuity, others claim modern innovation.

Label:
"Contested Authority"@en

Concept Schemes

Access Level Classification

https://iroko-framework.github.io/iroko-framework/vocab/iroko-core#AccessLevelScheme

Hierarchical classification of access restrictions for sacred knowledge.

Community Only Rank: 3
Accessible to verified community members (practitioners, scholars with community relationships). Requires authentication.
Initiated Elder Rank: 5
Requires elder designation within tradition — those recognized by their community as having authority to access lineage-specific teachings, complex ceremonial knowledge, and restricted spiritual technologies. Not merely initiated, but recognized as elder or authority holder.
Initiated Only Rank: 4
Accessible only to initiated practitioners whose initiation has been verified. Detailed preparation methods, quantities, combinations.
No Access Rank: 99
Operational or internal use only. Never exported to RDF. Invocation texts, secret names, internal notes.
Public - No Amplification Rank: 2
Publicly viewable but should not be amplified, shared widely, or used for commercial purposes.
Public - Unrestricted Rank: 1
Freely accessible to anyone. No authentication required.

Assertion Status

https://iroko-framework.github.io/iroko-framework/vocab/iroko-core#AssertionStatusScheme

Controlled terms describing whether a claim is widely agreed, lineage-specific, or actively disputed. Used with iroko:RelationshipAssertion and any contested property across all modules.

Contested The claim is actively disputed; multiple contradictory positions exist across lineages.
Lineage-Specific The claim is recognized within a specific lineage or house but not universally.
Widely Attested The claim is affirmed by multiple independent lineages or sources. Positive attestation of agreement, not merely the absence of known dispute. Also: Cross-lineage consensus, Uncontested

Community Endorsement Status

https://iroko-framework.github.io/iroko-framework/vocab/iroko-core#CommunityEndorsementScheme

Classification of community review and approval status.

Community-Reviewed Reviewed by community members but not yet formally approved by elders.
Contested Disputed across community; multiple contradictory positions exist.
Elder-Approved Formally approved by recognized community elders or authorities.
Pending Review Under active review by community authorities.
Unreviewed Has not yet been reviewed by community authorities.
Withdrawn Previously endorsed but subsequently withdrawn by community.

Consent Status

https://iroko-framework.github.io/iroko-framework/vocab/iroko-core#ConsentStatusScheme

Community-Authorized Recording authorized by community leadership or house authority on behalf of participants.
Documented Consent Written or formally recorded consent obtained from subject(s) and/or community.
Not Obtained No consent was sought or obtained. Access should be governed accordingly.
Unknown Consent status cannot be determined from available documentation.
Verbal (Undocumented) Consent was given verbally but not formally documented.

Custodial Relationship

https://iroko-framework.github.io/iroko-framework/vocab/iroko-core#CustodialRelationshipScheme

Classification of custodial arrangements for sacred objects.

Community-Held Object held and controlled by originating community.
Institutional Holding Disputed Institution holds object; community disputes legitimacy of custody.
Institutional Holding with Community Access Institution holds object but community maintains access and consultation rights.
Lineage Succession Object received through lineage or house transmission. The current custodian holds the object by virtue of succession from a prior keeper through recognized lineage or house relationships. Paired with sacredStatus-hereditary-custody on the object record.
Private Collection Held in private collection, access restricted.
Repatriation Claim Active Active claim for repatriation to community of origin.
Shared Custody Custody shared between institution and community through formal agreement.
Under Negotiation Custody arrangement currently under negotiation.
Unknown Location Current location unknown; object documented but whereabouts unclear.

Epistemic Source Type

https://iroko-framework.github.io/iroko-framework/vocab/iroko-core#EpistemicTypeScheme

Classification of knowledge sources for epistemological transparency.

Archival Document Historical documents (manuscripts, letters, official records).
Claimed Direct Revelation Knowledge claimed to be received through direct spiritual revelation or channeling.
Continuous Oral Transmission Knowledge transmitted orally across generations without interruption.
Fieldwork Recording Direct ethnographic observation and documentation by researchers.
Material Culture Knowledge inferred from physical objects, ritual implements, or architecture.
Political Reinterpretation Deliberate reinterpretation of existing tradition for political, social, or liberation purposes.
Published Ethnography Knowledge documented in academic or popular ethnographic publications.
Reconstructed from Text Knowledge reconstructed from ancient texts or historical documents without living transmission.
Ritual Performance (Embodied) Knowledge transmitted through embodied practice, dance, gesture, and music.
Speculative Reconstruction Theoretical reconstruction based on fragmentary evidence or comparative analysis.

Sacred Object Status

https://iroko-framework.github.io/iroko-framework/vocab/iroko-core#SacredStatusScheme

Classification of ritual life cycle status for sacred objects.

Awaiting Consecration Object prepared but not yet consecrated.
Consecrated/Active Use Object is consecrated and actively used in ritual practice.
Contested Consecration Consecration status disputed across lineages.
Decommissioned Object has been ritually deactivated or decommissioned but retains historical significance.
Hereditary Custody Object has passed to a new keeper through lineage or house succession and is actively maintained. Custodial responsibility has transferred but sacred status and care protocols remain continuous.
Honored Rest Object has been retired from active ritual use but continues to be tended with respect and veneration. Distinguished from decommissioned in that no formal ritual deactivation has occurred — the object rests rather than being withdrawn.
Museum Specimen Object held in museum or archive for documentation, not ritual use.
Replica/Teaching Object Replica created for educational purposes, never consecrated.
Unknown Status Sacred status unknown or unclear from available documentation.

Tradition Classification

https://iroko-framework.github.io/iroko-framework/vocab/iroko-core#TraditionScheme

Controlled vocabulary of Afro-Atlantic tradition classifications.

21 Divisiones Dominican syncretic tradition with Vodou and Espiritismo influences. Also: Dominican Vodu
Abakua Calabari/Efik-derived all-male secret society in Cuba.
Afro-Indigenous Traditions that blend African and Indigenous American religious elements.
Akan-Derived Traditions with primary roots in Akan religious culture.
Arara Fon/Ewe-derived tradition in Cuba.
Ausar Auset Society Kemetic-centered spiritual organization founded by Ra Un Nefer Amen.
Bantu-Derived Traditions with primary roots in Bantu and Kongo religious culture.
