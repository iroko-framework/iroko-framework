Manifestation

Manifestation Module — Sacred Agent Modes

Manifestation modes and media: possession, dream, divination, symbolic presence

mani:  https://www.irokosociety.org/iroko-framework/manifestation# v2.0.0 Download TTL ↓ GitHub ↗

Governance Layer module. Optional module for modeling how sacred agents are asserted to manifest — through embodied presence, possession, dream, divination, symbolic representation, territorial claim, auditory sign, or unknown mode. Designed to plug into ag:ManifestationEvent. Supports iroko:TemporalVariation where manifestation modes shift by ritual calendar or initiation cycle. Use with iroko-narrative to link manifestation events to the narrative records in which they are described, and iroko-authority to document which lineage context authorizes the assertion.

Manifestation Modes 8 concepts

Controlled terms for modes in which sacred agents are asserted to manifest. These are descriptive categories that may vary by tradition, lineage, or temporal regime.

Auditory
mode-auditory

Manifestation asserted to occur through sound, voice, or auditory sign.

Divination
mode-divination

Manifestation asserted to occur through divination, oracle, or interpretive instrument.

Dream
mode-dream

Manifestation asserted to occur through dream or dream visitation.

Embodied
mode-embodied

Manifestation asserted to occur in embodied, physical form.

Possession
mode-possession

Manifestation asserted to occur through possession or mounting of a human medium.

Symbolic
mode-symbolic

Manifestation asserted to occur through symbols, signs, or indirect representation.

Territorial
mode-territorial

Manifestation asserted to be bound to or expressed through a specific place, site, or territory.

Unknown
mode-unknown

Manifestation mode not known or not recorded in available sources.

Medium Types 6 concepts

Controlled terms for types of media through which sacred agent manifestation is asserted to occur.

Instrument
medium-instrument

A musical, divinatory, or ritual instrument serves as the medium.

Object
medium-object

A material object (vessel, tool, consecrated item) serves as the medium.

Person
medium-person

A human person serves as the medium of manifestation.

Place
medium-place

A place, site, natural feature, or territory serves as the medium.

Text
medium-text

A text, inscription, verse, or document serves as the medium.

Unknown
medium-unknown

Medium type not known or not recorded in available sources.
